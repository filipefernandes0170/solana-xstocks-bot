Terms

What the words mean

Fair
Nasdaq is open, a $1,000 buy is within 0.4% of the stock, the issuer mark agrees, and the buy does not move the price by 1% or more.
Near
The session is open and the buy is not far, but it missed Fair. Read the gap before a larger order.
Careful
The session is open, but a $1,000 buy is at least 1% off the print, or it moves the price by 2% or more.
Wait
The session is closed, or the name has no around-the-clock quote. The next open usually pulls the token back toward the print.
Overnight
The session is closed, but Kraken still quotes this name. It can look close. It is still not the Nasdaq print.
Split
The pool and the issuer mark disagree, and those two gaps cancel, so the token looks close to the stock. The buy only catches the pool.
Checking
A price or the issuer mark is still loading. This is not a buy signal.
No print
There is no public stock price to judge the token against.
Rich
On the gap pill, the buy is more than 0.02% above the stock. A name can be a little rich and still be Fair, because Fair allows up to 0.4%.
Cheap
On the gap pill, the buy is more than 0.02% below the stock. Cheap is not a promise that the token will rise.
Flat
On the gap pill, the buy is within 0.02% of the stock.
Gap
How far a $1,000 buy is from the Nasdaq print. It is not the last trade, and it is not your profit.
Share meter
One raw token times the multiplier is the number of shares. The pool prices the raw token. Dividing by the multiplier is the price per share.
Multiplier
The number on the mint. It starts at 1. A reinvested dividend or a split raises it. A reverse split lowers it. Your raw tokens do not change.
Raw token
The amount the chain stores. Wallets that know the multiplier show shares instead. A pool trades the raw token.
